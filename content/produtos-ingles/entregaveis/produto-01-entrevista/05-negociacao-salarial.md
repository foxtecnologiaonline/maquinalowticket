# Módulo 5 — Negociação Salarial em Inglês (Scripts Prontos)

## Regra de ouro
Nunca dê o primeiro número se puder evitar — jogue a pergunta de volta primeiro.

**Quando perguntarem sua pretensão logo na triagem:**
> "Could you share the budgeted range for this role? That would help me give you a more accurate answer based on the full scope of responsibilities."

**Se insistirem para você dar um número:**
> "Based on my research and experience level, I'd expect something in the range of [X to Y], but I'm flexible depending on the full package — benefits, growth path, and role scope."

## Quando você recebe uma proposta abaixo do esperado

> "Thank you for the offer, I'm really excited about the opportunity. That said, based on my experience and the market range for this role, I was expecting something closer to [valor]. Is there flexibility on the base salary?"

## Quando a empresa diz que não há flexibilidade no salário-base

> "I understand budget constraints. In that case, could we explore other parts of the package — a signing bonus, additional PTO, remote flexibility, or a compensation review at 6 months?"

## Negociando com múltiplas propostas (alavancagem)

> "I want to be transparent — I'm currently in the final stages with another company. This role is my top choice, but the other offer is at [valor/benefício]. Is there room to make this offer more competitive?"

## Aceitando a proposta

> "Thank you, I'm happy to accept the offer at [valor combinado]. Could you send the formal offer letter so I can review the final details?"

## Erros comuns a evitar

1. Aceitar no mesmo minuto sem pedir tempo para revisar ("Can I have 24-48 hours to review the offer?").
2. Focar só no salário-base e ignorar bônus, equity, benefícios, PTO.
3. Justificar o pedido só por "necessidade pessoal" — sempre ancorar em mercado/experiência/resultados.
4. Ser agressivo demais — o tom é sempre colaborativo, nunca de confronto.

## Frases de transição úteis durante toda a negociação

- "I appreciate the offer and I'm very interested in moving forward."
- "I just want to make sure we're aligned before I accept."
- "Is there any flexibility here?"
- "What would it take to get to [valor]?"
