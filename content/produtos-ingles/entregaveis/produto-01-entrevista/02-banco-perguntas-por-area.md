# Módulo 2 — Banco de Perguntas por Área

Cada área tem 12 perguntas técnicas/comportamentais específicas + resposta modelo (framework STAR: Situation, Task, Action, Result).

## Framework STAR (use em toda pergunta comportamental)

- **Situation**: contexto breve
- **Task**: qual era seu objetivo/desafio
- **Action**: o que você fez (foco aqui, é a parte mais longa)
- **Result**: resultado mensurável

---

## TI / Desenvolvimento

1. "Describe your experience with [linguagem/framework]."
2. "Tell me about a time you had to debug a critical production issue."
   > STAR: "Our checkout API started returning 500 errors in production (S). I needed to identify and fix the root cause without further downtime (T). I checked recent deploys, isolated the issue to a database connection leak, and rolled back while preparing a permanent fix (A). We restored service in 20 minutes and shipped the fix the next day, preventing recurrence (R)."
3. "How do you approach code review?"
4. "Explain a technical decision you disagreed with and how you handled it."
5. "How do you keep your code maintainable in a fast-moving team?"
6. "Describe your experience with CI/CD pipelines."
7. "How do you handle technical debt?"
8. "Tell me about a project where you had to learn a new technology quickly."
9. "How do you approach testing?"
10. "Describe a time you improved system performance."
11. "How do you communicate technical concepts to non-technical stakeholders?"
12. "What's your experience working in Agile/Scrum teams?"

## Vendas / Comercial

2. "Tell me about your biggest sale/deal."

I researched their recent expansion and tailored a proposal around it, then set up a live demo focused on their specific pain point (A). We closed a $[X] annual contract, our largest that quarter (R)."

4. "Describe how you build a pipeline from scratch."
5. "How do you handle a customer who says the price is too high?"
6. "Tell me about a time you exceeded your quota."
7. "How do you qualify a lead?"
8. "Describe your experience with CRM tools."
9. "How do you handle a long sales cycle with multiple stakeholders?"
10. "Tell me about a time you lost a deal. What did you learn?"
11. "How do you stay motivated during a slow month?"
12. "What's your approach to upselling/cross-selling existing clients?"

## Marketing

1. "Tell me about a campaign you led from start to finish."
2. "How do you measure the success of a campaign?"
3. "Describe a time a campaign underperformed. What did you do?"
   > STAR: "We launched a paid campaign that had a much lower CTR than projected (S). I needed to identify the issue and improve performance within the remaining budget (T). I ran an audit on targeting and creative, found the audience was too broad, and split-tested three new segments (A). CTR improved by 40% and we hit our lead target by the campaign's end (R)."
4. "How do you approach A/B testing?"
5. "What tools do you use for analytics/reporting?"
6. "How do you work with sales to align on lead quality?"
7. "Describe your experience with content strategy."
8. "How do you approach SEO?"
9. "Tell me about a time you had to work with a limited budget."
10. "How do you stay updated on marketing trends?"
11. "Describe your experience with paid social vs. paid search."
12. "How do you define your target audience for a new product?"

## Como adaptar para outras áreas

1. Pegue as 3 perguntas comportamentais genéricas do Kit Grátis (perguntas 7, 9, 10).
2. Substitua o contexto pelo cenário típico da sua área.
3. Sempre estruture a resposta em STAR, terminando com um número/resultado.
