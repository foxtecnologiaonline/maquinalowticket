# Kit de Sobrevivência: As 20 Frases que Decidem sua Entrevista em Inglês

*Guia gratuito — Método D.I.R. (Diagnóstico → Imersão em Cenário Real → Repetição com Feedback)*

## Como usar este guia

1. Leia cada pergunta e a resposta modelo em voz alta, 3 vezes.
2. Grave você mesmo respondendo, sem olhar o texto na 3ª vez.
3. Ouça e compare com o áudio de pronúncia (link enviado por e-mail).
4. Repita por 5 dias seguidos, 15 minutos por dia.

---

## Bloco 1 — Abertura (quebra-gelo)

**1. "Tell me about yourself."**
> *Modelo:* "I'm a [sua profissão] with [X] years of experience in [área]. Currently, I work at [empresa] where I [principal responsabilidade/resultado]. I'm looking for a new opportunity where I can [objetivo, ex: grow into a leadership role / apply my skills in a bigger market]."

**2. "Why are you interested in this position?"**
> *Modelo:* "I've been following [empresa] for a while and I admire [motivo específico]. This role matches exactly what I want to do next in my career, especially [ponto técnico da vaga]."

**3. "Why do you want to leave your current job?"**
> *Modelo:* "I've learned a lot in my current role, but I'm looking for new challenges, especially in [área/tecnologia]. I feel I've reached a point where I want to grow further, and this position offers that."

**4. "Walk me through your resume."**
> *Modelo:* "Sure. I started my career at [empresa 1] as a [cargo], where I [conquista]. Then I moved to [empresa 2], focusing on [área]. Currently, at [empresa atual], I [responsabilidade principal]."

---

## Bloco 2 — Competências e Pontos Fortes

**5. "What are your strengths?"**
> *Modelo:* "One of my key strengths is [força, ex: problem-solving]. For example, at my last job, I [exemplo concreto com resultado numérico se possível]."

**6. "What is your biggest weakness?"**
> *Modelo:* "I used to struggle with [fraqueza real, mas controlável], but I've been working on it by [ação concreta], and I've already seen improvement."

**7. "Give an example of a time you solved a difficult problem."**
> *Modelo:* "In my previous role, we faced [problema]. I [ação que você tomou], which resulted in [resultado mensurável]."

**8. "How do you handle pressure or tight deadlines?"**
> *Modelo:* "I prioritize tasks based on impact and deadline, and I communicate early if something is at risk. For example, [exemplo real]."

**9. "Describe a time you worked in a team."**
> *Modelo:* "I collaborated with [equipe/área] on [projeto]. My role was to [responsabilidade], and together we achieved [resultado]."

**10. "Tell me about a time you made a mistake. What did you learn?"**
> *Modelo:* "Early in my career, I [erro real e não-crítico]. I learned the importance of [lição], and since then I always [nova prática]."

---

## Bloco 3 — Sobre a Vaga e a Empresa

**11. "What do you know about our company?"**
> *Modelo:* "[Empresa] is known for [diferencial], and recently I read about [notícia/produto/expansão]. I'm especially interested in [ponto específico]."

**12. "Why should we hire you?"**
> *Modelo:* "I bring [X years] of hands-on experience in [área], with a track record of [resultado concreto]. I also adapt quickly to new environments, which I believe fits well with [algo da cultura da empresa]."

**13. "What are your salary expectations?"**
> *Modelo:* "Based on my research and experience level, I'd expect something in the range of [faixa]. But I'm open to discussing based on the full compensation package."

**14. "Where do you see yourself in 5 years?"**
> *Modelo:* "I see myself growing into a more senior/leadership role, having deepened my expertise in [área], and contributing to bigger projects within the company."

**15. "Do you have experience working with international teams?"**
> *Modelo:* "Yes, I've worked with colleagues from [países/regiões] on [projeto], communicating mainly through [ferramentas/canais], which helped me get comfortable with different work styles and time zones."

---

## Bloco 4 — Perguntas Técnicas Genéricas (adapte à sua área)

**16. "What tools/technologies are you most comfortable with?"**
> *Modelo:* "I'm most experienced with [ferramentas], and I've also worked with [ferramenta secundária] for [caso de uso]."

**17. "How do you stay updated in your field?"**
> *Modelo:* "I follow [fontes: blogs, newsletters, comunidades], and I also take courses on [plataforma] to keep my skills current."

---

## Bloco 5 — Fechamento

**18. "Do you have any questions for us?"** (nunca responda "no")
> *Modelo:* "Yes — could you tell me more about how success is measured in this role in the first 6 months?" / "What does the team structure look like?"

**19. "What is your availability to start?"**
> *Modelo:* "I would need to give a [X-week] notice to my current employer, but I'm flexible and can discuss a start date that works for both sides."

**20. "Is there anything else you'd like us to know about you?"**
> *Modelo:* "Just that I'm genuinely excited about this opportunity, and I believe my experience in [área] combined with my adaptability would add real value to the team."

## Checklist — Erros que Eliminam seu Currículo em 5 Segundos

- [ ] Nome de arquivo genérico (`curriculo.pdf`) → use `Nome_Sobrenome_Cargo.pdf`
- [ ] E-mail pouco profissional → use `nome.sobrenome@email.com`
- [ ] Resumo/objetivo em português misturado com inglês
- [ ] Verbos de ação fracos ("responsible for") em vez de fortes ("led", "delivered", "increased")
- [ ] Ausência de números/resultados (%, R$, tempo economizado)
- [ ] Mais de 2 páginas para quem tem menos de 10 anos de experiência
- [ ] Formatação inconsistente (fontes, tamanhos, espaçamento)
